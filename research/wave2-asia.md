## 1. Pacing and restraint — the time dimension

1. **One or two house curves for the whole site** — SHIFTBRAIN ships four curves in total and uses them by role: `cubic-bezier(.43,.05,.17,1)` (×79, long reveals), `cubic-bezier(.18,.06,.23,1)` (×69, opacity), `cubic-bezier(.55,.05,.22,.99)` (×47, transforms), `cubic-bezier(.26,.06,0,1)` (×25) — unity of motion reads as one author; a mixed bag of defaults reads as a template — https://shiftbrain.com/ `[verified-src]`
2. **The same rule stated as a rule** — "set the same easing everywhere and the site gains unity"; the "smooth / rich" recipe is `cubic-bezier(0.55, 0.05, 0.22, 0.99)` at 0.7s for transforms and `cubic-bezier(0.46, 0.1, 0.29, 1)` at 0.3s for short ones — a named feeling mapped to a curve is reproducible — https://www.evoworx.co.jp/blog/transition_easing/ `[verified]`
3. **That exact "rich" curve is a de-facto Tokyo standard** — `cubic-bezier(.55,.05,.22,.99)` appears ×112 in QUOITWORKS and ×47 in SHIFTBRAIN — it is a soft in-out with a long tail: starts without a jerk, lands slowly — https://quoitworks.com/ , https://shiftbrain.com/ `[verified-src]`
4. **Transform slow, opacity fast and linear** — monopo: `transition: all 1.2s cubic-bezier(.19,1,.22,1)` for movement, but `opacity .2s–.5s linear`; STUDIO DETAILS: `opacity .4s linear, transform 1.6s var(--ease-out-expo)` — an eased fade stalls near 0 and 1 and looks like a hang; the element becomes readable in 0.4s while the body keeps settling for 1.6s — https://monopo.co.jp/ , https://www.details.co.jp/ `[verified-src]`
5. **Why fades are linear** — ICS MEDIA: use `linear` with a shorter duration for fades, otherwise an unintended pause appears near 0% and 100% opacity — https://ics.media/entry/18730/ `[verified]`
6. **Default when unsure: easeOutExpo** — "迷ったら easeOutExpo": `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`; ICS lists the older variant `cubic-bezier(0.19, 1, 0.22, 1)` and easeOutQuint `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` — out-curves answer the user instantly and leave a tail ("余韻", lingering) — https://zenn.dev/nishinoshake/articles/9fbef6c95eeac9 , https://ics.media/entry/18730/ `[verified]`
7. **Never ease-in on a response** — ease-in on a user-triggered change is perceived as lag; reserve ease-in for exits and for non-interactive sequences — https://ics.media/entry/18730/ , https://note.com/sagaraya/n/nd4a2c43ba87b `[verified]`
8. **Long is normal: 1.4s–2s reveals** — The Shift (Garden Eight) has `2s` ×43 in its transitions; Garden Eight's own site uses `1.4s` ×16; izanami-official.com (baqemono) has GSAP `duration:1.4` ×18 with `quart.out` ×11 — Western UI guidance sits at 0.2–0.5s; these sites triple it for anything that is not a direct response, and the slowness itself is the luxury signal — https://theshift.tokyo/ , https://garden-eight.com/ , https://izanami-official.com/ `[verified-src]`
9. **WebGL state changes are slower still: 2–4.5s, symmetric** — homunculus drives shader uniforms with `Power2.easeInOut` over 2s, 2.5s, 3s, 3.5s, 4s and 4.5s (e.g. blur power → 0 in 4.5s, colour power → 0 in 3.5s, distortion strength → .04 in 2s); in-out ×57 vs out ×41 — a 3D scene has mass; in-out gives it inertia at both ends, where DOM uses out — https://homunculus.jp/ (assets/js/main.js) `[verified-src]`
10. **Delay before the scene moves** — the same file starts several scene tweens with `delay:1` (alpha to .8 over 3s after 1s; speed to min over 4s after 1s) — one full second of stillness after a transition is "ma": the pause is what makes the following movement register — https://homunculus.jp/ `[verified-src]`
11. **"Ma" as a design tool, stated** — necco: the storyteller's speed and pause map directly to `animation-timing-function` and `delay`; ease-out's deceleration carries the sense "it has arrived"; a 500–1000ms silence before a reply creates tension — use on purpose, never by accident — https://necco.inc/note/55786/ `[verified]`
12. **Asymmetry: appear slowly, disappear instantly** — necco's rule; aircord encodes the opposite asymmetry for hover: `--a-btn-hover-leave-s: calc(var(--a-btn-hover-enter-s)*1.5)` (enter .6s, leave .9s, both power4-out) — whichever direction you choose, in and out must not be mirror images — https://necco.inc/note/55786/ , https://aircord.co.jp/ `[verified]` / `[verified-src]`
13. **Game-UI frame budgets used by Japanese motion designers** — appear in 6–15 frames, disappear in 4–8 frames; "keep the strongly accelerating part short and the strongly decelerating part long" — gives a numeric floor for micro-UI even when the page itself is slow — https://grow-ui-guild.gameanimation.info/uianimation_basic-3/ `[verified]`
14. **Response in 0.1s, then stillness** — 0.1s of immediate reaction followed by ~0.3s of settling; screen transitions about 0.5s; self-starting messages that must be read about 3.5s — separates "I heard you" from "here is the show" — https://note.com/sagaraya/n/nd4a2c43ba87b `[verified]`
15. **Stagger in coarse 0.3s steps on long tweens** — SHIFTBRAIN: `transition: all 1.7s cubic-bezier(.43,.05,.17,1)` with delays `.3s / .6s / .9s / 1.2s / 1.5s`, and `opacity 2s` with `.6 / .9 / 1.2 / 1.5 / 1.8s` — each line is still moving when the next starts; the block reads as one breath, not five pops — https://shiftbrain.com/ `[verified-src]`
16. **Fine stagger in 0.05s steps for UI clusters** — Garden Eight delays `.2 / .25 / .3 / .35 / .4 / .45 / .5s`; The Shift `.1 / .15 / .2 / .3 / .5 / .54 / .6 / .66 / .72s` (note the irregular 0.06 steps) — https://garden-eight.com/ , https://theshift.tokyo/ `[verified-src]`
17. **Stagger from a grid index, not a list index** — STUDIO DETAILS: `--_on-screen-delay: calc(var(--_i) * var(--_column-interval) + var(--_row-index) * var(--_row-interval) + var(--_base-delay))` — the wave travels diagonally across a grid; ICS's variant uses index-squared delay for a clip reveal — https://www.details.co.jp/ , https://ics.media/entry/250221/ `[verified-src]` / `[verified]`
18. **Split opacity and translate into different durations and curves** — ICS "advanced" fade-up: translate `0 13%`, duration 1.8s, opacity on `cubic-bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1)`, translate on `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`; per-character version: opacity 0.7s, translate 1.4s — the eye gets the content early, the body gets the tail — https://ics.media/entry/250221/ `[verified]`
19. **Trigger low in the viewport** — same article: `rootMargin: "-80% 0px -20%"`… the basic version triggers at centre (`-50% 0px`) and the advanced one in the lower part so that text is already readable when it reaches centre — https://ics.media/entry/250221/ `[verified]`
20. **Focus-pull entrance** — scale 1.1 → 1, blur `1em` → 0, opacity 0 → 1; 1s for scale and opacity, 1.4s for blur — the blur outlasting the fade reads as a lens finding focus — https://ics.media/entry/250221/ `[verified]`
21. **Mist-condensing headline** — 4.5s ease-out; `opacity 0 → 1`, `filter: blur(14px) → 0`, `letter-spacing: .6em → .18em`; use once per page (letter-spacing animates layout) — https://necco.inc/note/85096/ `[verified]`
22. **Offset parameters on purpose** — animate X and Y with different easings (quartInOut vs backOut) and offset one axis by 0.1s; a scale squash that starts 450ms into a 450ms fall with `outBack(4)` — "handling each parameter separately gives a different flavour" — https://ics.media/entry/14346/ `[verified]`
23. **Meaning before motion** — SHIFTBRAIN interactive developers: it is not always right to move things dramatically; every site needs its own fitting movement; they collect references from film titles, TV ads, street displays, even folk-dance video — https://note.com/shiftbrain/n/n4ef08123ac78 `[verified]`
24. **"Lingering movement and the mouse pointer are an important part of the presentation"** — Garden Eight on The Shift; inertia and direction changes of the archive scroll were tuned by hand — https://www.awwwards.com/the-shift-by-garden-eight-wins-site-of-the-month-october-2021.html `[verified]`
25. **Jo-ha-kyu as a scroll script** — slow opening (jo), development with breaks in rhythm (ha), fast resolution (kyu): give the first screen the longest timings (2–4s), the middle chapters 1–1.4s, and the final CTA the shortest (0.4–0.6s) — no web source was found that states this for sites; the verified timings above (items 8–10) are consistent with it — `[recalled]`

## 2. Micro-interaction recipes

26. **Arrow that leaves and re-enters (mount)** — on hover the icon flies out: `transition: transform cubic-bezier(0.1, 0, 1, 0.5) .15s; transform: translate3d(70%,0,0)`; then it is placed at `-100%` and returns with `transition: transform cubic-bezier(0, 0.5, 0.5, 1) .75s` — exit 0.15s ease-in, entry 0.75s ease-out: a 1:5 asymmetry that feels mechanical and precise — https://mount.jp/ (common.css) `[verified-src]`
27. **Five-second settle (mount)** — in-page anchor arrow: `transition: translate 5s cubic-bezier(0, 1, 0, 1), bottom 5s cubic-bezier(0, 1, 0, 1)` — an extreme expo-out: 90% of the travel in the first moments, then a multi-second creep; nobody sees it consciously, everybody feels "alive" — https://mount.jp/ `[verified-src]`
28. **Near-instant hover (baqemono)** — `--hover-duration-default: .05s; --hover-ease-default: linear; --hover-text-opacity: .67` — the opposite school: hover is feedback, not a show; 50ms linear to 67% opacity. Pick one school per site — https://baqemono.jp/ `[verified-src]`
29. **Hover tokens with slower leave (aircord)** — `--a-btn-hover-enter-s:.6s`, leave `×1.5`, both `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`; menu open 1.2s — https://aircord.co.jp/ `[verified-src]`
30. **Per-character hover roll without extra DOM** — each letter gets `style="--index: n"`; `transition-delay: calc(var(--index) * 0.05s)`, duration 0.2s; the "second copy" of the letter is a `text-shadow: 0 -1.5em 0 #000` that swaps to `0 1.5em 0`; wrap in `aria-hidden="true" translate="no"` and add a `.sr-only` duplicate — https://ics.media/entry/250131/ `[verified]`
31. **Split-text reveal with rotation (aircord)** — tokens `--a-split-s:2.2s; --a-split-e:power4-out; --a-split-y:1em; --a-split-deg:1deg` — lines rise 1em with a 1° tilt over 2.2s; one degree is enough to stop it looking like a slide — https://aircord.co.jp/ `[verified-src]`
32. **Slide-in text with per-line offset (STUDIO DETAILS)** — `--_slide-in-text-duration: 1.2s; --_slide-in-text-ease: cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, line delays `base`, `base + .3s`, `base + .6s` — https://www.details.co.jp/ `[verified-src]`
33. **Pop-in letters** — translate `0 100% → 0`, scale `.7 → 1`; both 0.4s; scale on `cubic-bezier(0.3, 3, 0.64, 1)`, translate on easeOutCubic; scale delayed `0.15s + 0.05s × index`, translate `0.05s × index` (uses independent `translate`/`scale` properties so no wrapper spans) — https://ics.media/entry/230309/ `[verified]`
34. **Rotating round CTA** — text ring `animation: rotate 5s linear infinite` plus `transition: scale .6s easeOutExpo`, hover `scale: 1.2` — https://ics.media/entry/230309/ `[verified]`
35. **Border that draws itself** — `.border .b { transform: scaleX(0); transform-origin: center left; transition: transform 2s cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) }` — hairlines are animated objects, 2s each — https://theshift.tokyo/ `[verified-src]`
36. **Image unveil** — `opacity: 0 → 1 in 1s` while `transform: scale(1.2) → 1 in 2s` — https://theshift.tokyo/ `[verified-src]`
37. **Negative-to-positive "development"** — entrance lasts 0.8s; shader `mix(1.0 - rgb, rgb, t)`; resets when the card leaves the viewport — https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/08/15/inside-haoqi-design-letting-dom-and-webgl-share-a-retro-futurist-stage/ `[verified]`
38. **Scramble text on one shared ticker** — characters cycle through capitals, digits and symbols before settling; a single 40ms ticker serves all instances; subscribe on viewport entry, unsubscribe on completion — same source `[verified]`
39. **One transition language for everything** — hover, full-screen transition and mobile menu all use the same rule: "continuous progress becomes the size of a shape on a fixed grid" (dot-matrix squares; `squareExtent = mix(0.0, 0.5, grow)`) — same source `[verified]`
40. **Magnetic button** — offset = pointer − element centre, multiplied by an ease coefficient; follow with `power1.out`, release with `back.out(4)`; use `gsap.quickTo`; disabled on touch — https://zenn.dev/takuma_hmng8/articles/fa72f8c7281698 `[verified]`
41. **Cursor that snaps to targets** — 20px circle; position tween 0.05s, morph to the hovered element's rect in 0.1s, optional trailing stalker 0.2s, `Power2.easeOut`; radius `min(w,h) * 0.1` — https://zenn.dev/aktriver/articles/2021-09-sticky-mouse-pointer `[verified]`
42. **Cursor as type** — aircord builds cursor icons with `TextGeometry` from an icon font instead of meshes; it saved about 1MB on mobile and lets the cursor state name the current function — https://www.awwwards.com/aircord-case-study.html `[verified]`
43. **Number counter** — ease-out quart: `const easeOutQuart = t => 1 - Math.pow(1 - t, 4)`; durations in the example 3000–5000ms; set `font-feature-settings: "tnum"` so digits do not jitter (STUDIO DETAILS ships `"tnum"`) — https://zenn.dev/nishinoshake/articles/9fbef6c95eeac9 , https://www.details.co.jp/ `[verified]` / `[verified-src]`
44. **List stagger** — 1s per item, 0.08s stagger — same Zenn article `[verified]`
45. **Loader tied to real progress** — Shapefarm: a Canvas 2D clip animation driven by the progress bar; all smoothing is "lerp + requestAnimationFrame" in own classes, no library — https://www.awwwards.com/technical-case-study-shapefarm-corporate-website.html `[verified]`
46. **Sound gate as the first decision** — homunculus opens on "Enter — with Sound / without Sound" (Howler loaded) and offers a subtitles toggle — a threshold ritual: the visitor chooses to enter — https://homunculus.jp/ `[verified-src]`
47. **Hidden keyboard switch** — Shapefarm toggles day/night theme with Shift+S; post-effect shader values depend on theme — https://www.awwwards.com/technical-case-study-shapefarm-corporate-website.html `[verified]`
48. **Blink and noise in steps** — den.cool `animation: blink .9s steps(1) infinite`; DFY `animation: noise 1s steps(4) infinite`; necco: `steps(1)` keeps a glitch mechanical, `ease` would make it unnaturally fluid — https://den.cool/ , https://dfy.co.kr/ , https://necco.inc/note/85096/ `[verified-src]` / `[verified]`
49. **Irregular flicker** — keyframes at 3%, 6%, 7%, 9%, 38–40%, 71–77% of a 4s loop, opacity dipping to .05–.3 — fear and attention come from not being able to predict the next dip; use for one status dot, not for text — https://necco.inc/note/85096/ `[verified]`
50. **Overshoot is a Korean accent** — Plus X ships `cubic-bezier(.31,1.69,.68,1)` ×85 next to easeOutCubic ×425 — a visible bounce on small UI; the Tokyo studios sampled have almost none. For a dark consultant site take the Tokyo side — https://www.plus-ex.com/ `[verified-src]`

## 3. Typography practice (and what transfers to Cyrillic)

51. **A strict type-to-leading grid (mount)** — only seven sizes: 12, 15, 18, 23, 28, 33, 43; leadings only 20, 30, 40: `12/20`, `15/30`, `18/30`, `23/40`, `28/40`, `33/40`, `43/40` — every text block lands on a 10-unit baseline; body 15/30 is leading 2.0 — https://mount.jp/ (common.css) `[verified-src]`
52. **The whole layout scales with the viewport** — `:root{font-size:calc(.0006944444*var(--vw))}` at ≥768px (1rem = 1/1440 of width) and `calc(.0024509804*var(--vw))` below (1/408) — the composition is a poster that is enlarged, not reflowed; proportions approved in the design file survive on every screen — https://mount.jp/ `[verified-src]`
53. **Tracking as four tokens** — baqemono: `--ls-narrower:.03em; --ls-narrow:.05em; --ls-wide:.1em; --ls-wider:.3em`; `--ls-wide` is the most used (×18) — https://baqemono.jp/ `[verified-src]`
54. **Positive tracking on body is the East-Asian default** — mount `.08em`, STUDIO DETAILS `.05em` (×51) and `.1em`, /zeroz `.1em`, izanami.co.jp `.1em` and `.33em`, tha `.05–.09em` — for Cyrillic body text use about half of that (`.01–.02em`), keep `.08–.12em` for small uppercase labels — sites as listed `[verified-src]`; the Cyrillic adjustment is `[recalled]`
55. **Negative tracking on Latin display only** — SHIFTBRAIN `-.005em … -.05em`; DFY `-.02 / -.03 / -.04 / -.06 / -.08em`; Plus X `-.057em`; monopo `-.03em` (×71) — tight display plus open body is the contrast pair — sites as listed `[verified-src]`
56. **Micro labels: 8–11px, mono, uppercase, .3–.38em** — daiki-design.com: `font-family: var(--font-mono); font-size: .52rem; letter-spacing: .3em; text-transform: uppercase` (scroll label), eyebrow `clamp(.55rem,.6vw,.68rem)` at `.38em` in gold `#C9A96E`; baqemono's second most common size is 10px (×11); junni copyright `clamp(8px, …, 11px)` — tiny wide-set labels are the cheapest way to make large type look larger — https://daiki-design.com/ , https://baqemono.jp/ , https://junni.co.jp/ `[verified-src]`
57. **Extreme tracking as a graphic** — Tao Tajima's name is set at `letter-spacing: 1em` (and `.4em`, `1.02em` elsewhere); Laugh Mind uses `1.2em` — a name becomes a row of points — https://taotajima.jp/ , https://laugh-mind.co.jp/ `[verified-src]`
58. **Body leading 1.8–2.5** — baqemono `1.923` and `2.5`; newfolk `1.8` (×28) and `2.4` (×13); izanami-official `1.8 / 2 / 2.4`; STUDIO DETAILS `2` (×32); utsubo 170–180% for Japanese — the government design system puts normal body at 150–175% — Cyrillic has no need for 2.5, but 1.7–1.8 on 15–16px in a narrow measure gives the same "air" — sites `[verified-src]`; https://design.digital.go.jp/dads/foundations/typography/ `[verified]`
59. **Small body, not big body** — baqemono's dominant size is 13px (×19); homunculus 14–16px; lqve.jp 12–13px (×40 combined); mount 15 units — premium Japanese sites keep body small and spend the scale contrast on headlines — https://baqemono.jp/ , https://lqve.jp/ `[verified-src]`
60. **Display line-height below 1** — DFY `.94` (×10); The Shift `.825em / .925em / .975em`; Garden Eight `.74` and `.51` for giant type — https://dfy.co.kr/ , https://theshift.tokyo/ , https://garden-eight.com/ `[verified-src]`
61. **Giant type in vw** — Garden Eight `14.93vw`, `17.916vw`; aircord `16.145vw`; The Shift `12.083vw` — one word spans the viewport, the rest is 13–15px — sites `[verified-src]`
62. **Script-specific size correction** — Garden Eight: `html{font-size:.972vw}` but `[lang=ja] .intro .p{font-size:1.041vw}` and `[lang=ja] .editor .j{font-size:1.006vw}` — the non-Latin script is re-sized so its optical size matches the Latin — do the same for Cyrillic vs Latin fragments (names, terms) — https://garden-eight.com/ `[verified-src]`
63. **Script-specific weight** — baqemono: `--font-weight-ja-default: 500` vs `--font-weight-en-default: 400` — two scripts at "the same weight" need different numeric weights; check Onest/Unbounded Cyrillic against any Latin display face the same way — https://baqemono.jp/ `[verified-src]`
64. **Composite font via @font-face** — give two fonts the same family name and split them with `unicode-range`; correct the smaller-looking one with `size-adjust` (example: kana at `size-adjust: 0.95`); stacking names in `font-family` is fallback, not composition — for this site: Latin + digits from one face, Cyrillic `U+0400-04FF` from another, one family name — https://zenn.dev/hidaruma/articles/e7e42097e9e802 `[verified]`
65. **How to find the size-adjust value** — divide one font's x-height by the other's and multiply by 100; published examples sit at 100–110% — https://code-plus.jp/gp/size-adjust-9463/ `[verified]`
66. **Pairing logic** — gothic with sans, mincho with serif; choose the secondary face with similar weight and width and a high x-height; the secondary script is often set slightly larger — https://note.com/h_s_2022/n/nc8e7f9fb692e `[verified]`
67. **Punctuation is typeset, not left to the font** — YakuHanJP placed first in the stack sets Japanese brackets and punctuation half-width (shipped by SHIFTBRAIN, QUOITWORKS, The Shift, aircord); `font-feature-settings: "palt"` for proportional kana (STUDIO DETAILS, QUOITWORKS, /zeroz) — Cyrillic equivalent: hang quotes «», non-breaking spaces after short prepositions, thin spaces around dashes, kerning on — https://yakuhanjp.qranoko.jp/ `[verified]`; usage `[verified-src]`; Cyrillic mapping `[recalled]`
68. **Case-sensitive forms on capitals** — /zeroz ships `font-feature-settings: "case" on` (×22) and `"case" on, "palt" on` (×18); DFY `"case" on, "salt" on` — brackets, dashes and colons align to cap height in uppercase labels — https://otsuka-air.jp/ , https://dfy.co.kr/ `[verified-src]`
69. **Vertical text as a margin label** — izanami-official: `.label{position:sticky; writing-mode:vertical-rl}`; QUOITWORKS: `writing-mode: vertical-lr; font-size:.7rem–.8rem; opacity:.3–.5; letter-spacing:.15rem`; junni: vertical copyright along the right edge; FunTech: vertical slogan pinned right; newfolk: a `7rem × 60.5rem` vertical quote — works in Cyrillic/Latin as rotated uppercase micro-labels (`writing-mode: vertical-rl` rotates Cyrillic glyphs sideways, which is the wanted look) — sites `[verified-src]`
70. **A vertical tag 34px wide** — STUDIO DETAILS role label: `width:34px; font-size:13px; line-height:1.2; letter-spacing:-.12em; background:#000; writing-mode:vertical-rl; text-orientation:mixed` — a black tab with vertical mincho, like a spine label — https://www.details.co.jp/ `[verified-src]`
71. **Three-voice font systems** — DFY: Faktum (grotesk) + Editorial Italic (serif italic) + YoonGothic (Hangul); izanami-official: Shippori Mincho + Cinzel + Playfair Display; Plus X: Roboto + Noto Sans KR + Libre Caslon Text; lqve: Suisse Intl + Suisse Works + ten-mincho-antique — one neutral sans, one serif (often italic) for emphasis, one native-script face — sites `[verified-src]`
72. **Light weights for display** — SHIFTBRAIN sets most text in TT Norms Light (×27) and ExtraLight (×15); tha uses Helvetica Neue Thin — thin large type on dark needs the off-white of item 105, not pure white — https://shiftbrain.com/ , https://tha.jp/ `[verified-src]`
73. **Typography ranked first** — "typography is the deepest differentiator" of Japanese web design; script mixing gives a rhythm inside a single line — https://www.utsubo.com/blog/japanese-web-design-style-guide `[verified]`
74. **Minimums** — the national design system forbids text below 14px for information and sets 16px as the body standard — decorative micro-labels (item 56) are fine below that only when they carry no information the page needs — https://design.digital.go.jp/dads/foundations/typography/ `[verified]`

## 4. WebGL craft

75. **Morph targets as extra attributes** — sample every shape with `MeshSurfaceSampler` to the same count (10,000 points in the tutorial), store as `position`, `secPosition`, `thirdPosition`…; vertex shader chains `mix(position, secPosition, u_sec1)` … one uniform per stage; call `.toNonIndexed()` before sampling — https://liginc.co.jp/553484 `[verified]`
76. **Scroll scrub with lag** — each stage: `scrollTrigger: { start: "bottom bottom", end: "bottom top", scrub: 0.7 }` — 0.7s of catch-up hides wheel steps — same source `[verified]`
77. **Morph timing when not scrubbed** — 4s `Power4.easeInOut` per shape in the EvoLab demo — https://evoworx.dev/lab/threejs-morphing/ `[verified]`
78. **Infinite-scroll progress split** — keep one continuous value; `current = floor(p)`, `next = floor(p + 1)`, blend by `fract(p)` in the shader; rest state is pulled to the nearest integer: `p += (round(p) - p) * 0.01` — with five shapes this gives magnetic "stations" without scroll-jacking — https://medium.com/@akella/taotajima-jp-webgl-deconstruction-af4946e8e8ed `[verified]`
79. **Curl noise, CPU version** — 2,400 particles; `noiseScale = 0.1`, `flowStrength = 0.003`, finite difference `E = 0.0001`; field sampled as `(noise(y,z,x), noise(z,x,y), noise(x,y,z))`; respawn when `length > 2 × range` — https://ics.media/entry/251120/ `[verified]`
80. **Curl noise, GPGPU version** — `GPUComputationRenderer`, 100×100 texture = 10,000 particles; `vel += snoiseDelta(pos * 3.0) * 0.1; vel *= 0.92; pos += vel * 0.01`; point size 2.0 — damping 0.92 is the "calm" knob — https://qiita.com/ukonpower/items/d0fee51bf3c181d4f71f `[verified]`
81. **Classic GPGPU layout (wgld.org / doxas)** — a float texture holds `xy = position, zw = velocity`; 512×512 = 262,144 particles; update: `v = normalize(mouse - p) * 0.2; w = normalize(v + vel); p += w * SPEED`, `SPEED = 0.05` — normalising the half-vector makes particles turn, not teleport — https://wgld.org/d/webgl/w083.html `[verified]`
82. **Glow stack for particles** — `UnrealBloomPass(res, 1.8, 0.8, 0.0)` then `AfterimagePass` with `damp = 0.86`; sprite is a 64px radial gradient white→transparent — afterimage gives trails for free — https://ics.media/entry/251120/ `[verified]`
83. **Node-based post chain (WebGPU/TSL)** — examples: chromatic aberration strength 0.2 followed by DoF with focus at 1; Gaussian blur 0.5; order sepia → blur → film — https://ics.media/entry/251113/ `[verified]`
84. **Depth of field for lines without a sharp core** — mount, KAI Design Dept.: render crisp lines to an FBO; a second FBO enlarges each line by its blur radius and encodes `G = blur area`, `R = blur intensity`; two-pass (vertical, horizontal) planar Gaussian; foreground and background drawn separately so crossings stay correct — "typical DoF on the web leaves a sharp core" — applies directly to thin connector lines between particle shapes — https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/11/20/behind-the-kai-design-dept-experience-webgl-line-blur-video-scrubbing-and-3d-animation/ `[verified]`
85. **DoF for points without a render pass** — Resn, Pioneer: thousands of GL points with distance-based scaling relative to a focal point and a signed-distance-field falloff for the blur, explicitly "avoided render passes" — https://www.awwwards.com/resns-pioneer-corn-revolutionized-wins-site-of-the-month-july-2020.html `[verified]`
86. **Text inside WebGL** — Resn renders most UI and text in WebGL with MSDF; outlines use stepped gradients; `fwidth`/`dFdx` keep stroke width constant — text can then be lit, blurred and occluded with the scene — same source `[verified]`
87. **Scroll velocity as a shader input** — smoothed scroll `cur = lerp(cur, target, 0.1)`; `offset = target - cur` drives vertex wave (amplitude 0.03) and RGB shift `offset * 0.0005` per channel (R full, G half, B none) — https://zenn.dev/bokoko33/articles/bd6744879af0d5 `[verified]`
88. **Fast attack, slow release on velocity** — HAOQI: velocity → strength with attack 0.025τ and release 0.175τ — the scene reacts at once and calms down seven times slower — https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/08/15/inside-haoqi-design-letting-dom-and-webgl-share-a-retro-futurist-stage/ `[verified]`
89. **Mouse and scroll speed drive RGB shift and noise** — Shapefarm's post-effects respond to pointer position and scroll speed; values differ per theme — https://www.awwwards.com/technical-case-study-shapefarm-corporate-website.html `[verified]`
90. **One frame loop for DOM and WebGL** — disable Lenis' own rAF, call `lenis.raf(time)` from the renderer's loop, publish one scroll snapshot per frame — removes the one-frame lag between text and canvas — HAOQI source above `[verified]`
91. **Lerp coefficients actually shipped** — Kenta Toshikura's site uses `ease: .025`, `.03`, `.075`, `.0875`, `.1`, `.175`, `.2`; /zeroz `lerpSpeed` `.08`, `.1`, `.12`, `.15`, `.2` — several followers with different coefficients on the same input create depth; 0.025 is the "heavy object" value — https://kentatoshikura.com/ , https://otsuka-air.jp/ `[verified-src]`
92. **Delta-time everything** — `timeRatio = dt / (1000/60)`; multiply every per-frame increment — particles must drift at the same speed at 60 and 120Hz — https://ics.media/entry/12930/ `[verified]`
93. **Soft particles** — depth pre-pass with `gl.colorMask(false,false,false,false)`; `fade = clamp(abs(particleDepth - sceneDepth) / max(uDepthFade, eps), 0., 1.)` — removes hard cuts where points meet geometry — https://techblog.kayac.com/webgl-threejs-soft-particle `[verified]`
94. **Layered render targets** — aircord: five layers (2D page → textured 3D plane → 2D screen → 3D world box → 2D UI) for "seamless transitions from 2D to 3D and back" — https://www.awwwards.com/aircord-case-study.html `[verified]`
95. **Background becomes content** — homunculus: the abstract pattern at the top distorts by shader into the first portfolio screenshot as you start to scroll; each screenshot moves at a slightly different scroll rate; the reviewer credits "the combined effect of small refinements" and a texture like "natural physical phenomena" — for particles: let the hero cloud become the first shape instead of cutting — https://webgl.souhonzan.org/entry/?v=2001 `[verified]`

## 6. Mobile-first handling of heavy WebGL

115. **Pixel-ratio caps actually shipped** — homunculus `Math.min(devicePixelRatio, 1.75)`; /zeroz (SHIFTBRAIN) mobile `Math.min(small ? 1.5 : 1.6, dpr)` where `small = screen.width*screen.height <= 310000`, desktop `Math.min(2, dpr)`; Shapefarm `Math.min(Math.max(dpr,1),2)` — nobody renders at 3 — https://homunculus.jp/ , https://otsuka-air.jp/ , https://shapefarm.net/ `[verified-src]`
116. **Why** — doubling canvas size is four times the fill work; Android devices may report a ratio of 3; apply selectively — https://webgl.souhonzan.org/entry/?v=0119 `[verified]`
117. **Antialias off** — `antialias: false` in /zeroz, izanami-official and Kenta Toshikura's renderer setup; the pixel ratio above 1.5 does the smoothing — sites `[verified-src]`
118. **Device score → resolution tier** — Kenta Toshikura computes a `spec.score` from retina flag, screen width and `navigator.hardwareConcurrency` (6+ or 8+ cores) and then sets `res` to 1, 1.25, 1.5 or 2 depending on window width (≥1920 → 1; ≥1880 → 1.25; ≥1680 → 1.5; else 2); tablets `min(dpr, 1.5)`; background layer always at `res = .5` — large windows get lower ratio, and the soft background layer is half-res everywhere — https://kentatoshikura.com/ `[verified-src]`
119. **What is dropped on touch devices** — same site: background layer only when `score > 7` and not a touch device; "contents" and "paper" layers are not initialised on any touch device — it removes whole layers rather than degrading all of them — https://kentatoshikura.com/ `[verified-src]`
120. **Mobile gets its own art direction, not a scaled copy** — /zeroz: helix radius 6 vs 9, turns .5 vs 1.32, plane width 420 vs 300, spot light intensity 3 vs 4.23, images scaled ×2.5, simpler material class on mobile — https://otsuka-air.jp/ `[verified-src]`
121. **Separate scroll physics for touch** — /zeroz Lenis: mobile `{ touchInertiaExponent: 1.6, syncTouchLerp: .065, syncTouch: page === "home" }`, desktop `{ duration: 1.2, autoRaf: false }` — synced touch only on the page that needs scrubbing — https://otsuka-air.jp/ `[verified-src]`
122. **Split bundles by device** — The Shift separates mobile and desktop JavaScript; aircord tree-shakes three.js and saved about 1MB of JS on mobile — https://www.awwwards.com/the-shift-by-garden-eight-wins-site-of-the-month-october-2021.html , https://www.awwwards.com/aircord-case-study.html `[verified]`
123. **Integrated-GPU detection** — read `UNMASKED_RENDERER_WEBGL` via `WEBGL_debug_renderer_info`; lower the pixel ratio for Intel onboard chips (rated the most effective of four measures) — https://ics.media/entry/12930/ `[verified]`
124. **Half-rate rendering as a last resort** — skip every second frame (`if (frame % 2 === 0) return;`); acceptable for slow ambient scenes, bad for anything interactive — https://ics.media/entry/12930/ `[verified]`
125. **Expensive passes at half resolution and half rate** — HAOQI's lens-flare pass runs at half resolution and refreshes every other frame; it is switched off when its source leaves the viewport; DOM rect sampling for far cards once per 12 frames — https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/08/15/inside-haoqi-design-letting-dom-and-webgl-share-a-retro-futurist-stage/ `[verified]`
126. **Magnetic and cursor effects off on touch** — the magnetic-button tutorial gates everything behind an `isTouch()` check — https://zenn.dev/takuma_hmng8/articles/fa72f8c7281698 `[verified]`
127. **Scrubbed video that works on phones** — mount: re-encode with a short keyframe interval `-g 12` (not all-intra), `-tune fastdecode -profile:v baseline -level 3.1 -movflags +faststart`; Firefox falls back to WebCodecs decoding — if a pre-rendered particle sequence replaces live WebGL on weak phones, this is the encode — https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/11/20/behind-the-kai-design-dept-experience-webgl-line-blur-video-scrubbing-and-3d-animation/ `[verified]`

## 8. What Asian premium sites do that Western ones usually do not

Each is a recipe. "Usually" is a judgement from this sample and from wave 1, not a measured share.

146. **Triple the durations for anything that is not a direct response** — reveals 1.2–2s, scene changes 2–4.5s, hover and press 0.05–0.6s. Set three CSS variables (`--t-ui: .3s; --t-reveal: 1.4s; --t-scene: 3s`) and allow nothing else. Basis: items 8, 9, 28, 29.
147. **Pause before the scene moves** — after each chapter change hold everything for 0.6–1s, then start the particle tween. Basis: items 10, 11.
148. **Decouple opacity from transform** — `opacity .4s linear, transform 1.6s cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`. Basis: items 4, 5, 18.
149. **In-out for the 3D, out for the DOM** — particles `power2.inOut`, text `expo.out`/`quart.out`. Basis: items 6, 9.
150. **Exit and entry are different animations** — exit 0.15s ease-in, entry 0.75s ease-out; or leave = enter × 1.5. Basis: items 12, 26, 29.
151. **Lock type to a leading grid** — seven sizes, three leadings, all multiples of one unit; body leading 1.7–2.0. Basis: items 51, 58.
152. **Scale the page as a poster** — `:root{font-size:calc(100vw/1440)}` with a separate mobile base (`100vw/408`), clamp for extremes. Basis: item 52.
153. **Open tracking on small text, tight on display** — labels `.3em` uppercase mono at 9–11px; headlines `−.02…−.05em`. Basis: items 53–57.
154. **Correct each script separately** — size, weight and tracking per `:lang()` or per `unicode-range` face; never trust one number for Cyrillic and Latin. Basis: items 62–65.
155. **Typeset punctuation** — dedicated handling for quotes, dashes, brackets; `"case"` on capitals, `"tnum"` on counters. Basis: items 43, 67, 68.
156. **Vertical micro-labels on the edges** — chapter name, index or copyright in `writing-mode: vertical-rl`, 10–12px, 30–50% opacity, sticky. Basis: items 69, 70, 110.
157. **Tinted black and tinted white** — background `#12–#21` with a hue, text `#D6D1CA`-like, line colour one step above background. Basis: items 102–105.
158. **Hierarchy by alpha, one accent** — four alphas of one text colour; accent used on one element type only. Basis: items 104, 106.
159. **A threshold ritual** — an explicit "enter" choice (sound on/off, or simply "Войти") before the experience starts; the loader is tied to real progress. Basis: items 45, 46.
160. **Hairlines are actors** — every rule draws in with `scaleX` over 1.5–2s from the left; in WebGL, lines get real depth blur. Basis: items 35, 84.
161. **One transition rule, three uses** — the same visual law for hover, section change and menu. Basis: item 39.
162. **Velocity with fast attack and slow release feeds the shader** — scroll speed → particle turbulence, RGB shift, stretch; attack ≈ 0.025, release ≈ 0.175. Basis: items 87–89.
163. **Several lerp speeds on one input** — cloud 0.025, camera 0.075, labels 0.1–0.2. Basis: item 91.
164. **Delete layers on phones instead of lowering everything** — keep the hero cloud and the type; remove the background layer, cursor effects and secondary passes; give mobile its own shape parameters. Basis: items 118–120, 126.
